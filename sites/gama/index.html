Questions people ask first

Will software built with AI help look generic or "AI-made"?+

It shouldn't. AI speeds up the building but doesn't make the decisions. Every site, app and system is designed and reviewed by a person for your business, your users and how you work.

I'm not technical at all. Will I understand what's happening?+

That's who GaMa is for. Every decision is explained in plain English, and you see progress as it happens.

Do I need an app, or would a website do the job?+

Often a website is all you need, and you'll be told so in the free chat rather than sold an app you don't need. An app makes sense when people use it regularly, need it offline, or want notifications and logins built in.

What's the difference between SEO and GEO?+

SEO gets you found when someone types a search into Google. GEO gets you mentioned when someone asks an AI tool like ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview a question instead. More of that searching now happens as a question to an AI, so the work covers both.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on what you need. A five-page website is very different from a custom Android app or an internal dashboard. Book a free chat and you'll get a straight number, not a "starting from" figure designed to get you in the door.

Is this only for medical and finance professionals?+

Doctors, therapists, accountants and consultants are a big part of it, because they are busy and careful about their online presence and their systems. Any self-employed professional or growing business that wants to look credible, run smoother and get found is a fit.

What if I already have a website?+

The site can be rebuilt, the marketing can be taken over while the site stays as it is, or the focus can go to an app or internal system instead. You'll get an honest view on which one you actually need.
