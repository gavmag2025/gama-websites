04.4 DECODING THE JARGON

"AI-built apps" and "GEO": what does that actually mean for you?

You've probably heard both phrases somewhere, on the news, from your nephew, in a LinkedIn post. Here's the honest, no-hype version.

AI-built means we can describe what an app, internal tool or piece of software needs to do in plain English, and AI helps us build it fast. That doesn't mean it's sloppy or "just AI." A human (us) still designs it, tests it, and makes sure it actually works for your business. You're simply not paying for six months of a developer typing code line by line.

GEO is the newer half: people are starting to ask ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview instead of typing a search query. GEO means making sure your business gets mentioned in that answer, the same way SEO makes sure you show up in a normal search.

08.5 BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions

Is an app or system built with AI help going to look "AI-made" or generic?+

It shouldn't, and here's why: AI speeds up the building, but it doesn't make the decisions. Every app or system is designed and reviewed by us for your business, your users and how you actually work, with the same care as a traditional build, just faster and cheaper.

I'm not technical at all. Will I understand what's happening?+

That's who we build for. No jargon, no "just trust the process." We explain every decision in plain English and show you progress as we go.

Do I need an app, or would a website actually do the job?+

Honestly, sometimes a website is all you need, and we'll tell you that in the free chat rather than talking you into an app you don't need. An app makes sense when people need to use it regularly, offline, or with things like notifications and logins baked in.

What's the difference between SEO and GEO, practically?+

SEO gets you found when someone types a search into Google. GEO gets you mentioned when someone asks an AI tool like ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview a question instead. More and more of that "search" is happening as a question to an AI, so we build for both.

How much does this cost?+

It depends on what you need. A five-page website is very different from a custom Android app or an internal dashboard. Book a free chat and we'll give you a straight, honest number, not a "starting from" figure designed to get you in the door.

Do you only work with medical and finance professionals?+

They're a big part of who we help, because they're often too busy (and too regulated) to gamble on their online presence or their internal systems. But we work with any self-employed professional or growing business that wants to look credible, run smoother, and get found.

What if I already have a website?+

We can rebuild it properly, take over the marketing and leave the site as-is, or focus entirely on an app or internal system instead. We'll tell you honestly which one you actually need.
